Privacy notice / Last updated 24 October 2026

Clear handling
for enquiry data.

This notice explains how Anna AI Studios handles information submitted through the studio contact form or sent through direct enquiry channels.

Contents Who controls the dataWhat we collectWhy we use itWho receives itHow long we keep itYour rightsSecurity and automationContact and complaints

Who controls the data

Anna AI Studios is an independent AI delivery studio based in London, United Kingdom. For privacy questions, email keke19870699@gmail.com. A branded privacy address will replace this contact when the studio’s custom domain and email service are activated.

What we collect

The enquiry form can collect your name, work email, company or team, project type, target timing, project brief, consent acknowledgement, source page, submission time and a generated lead reference. Direct email, WhatsApp or WeChat enquiries contain whatever information you choose to send through those services.

Do not send confidential production assets, credentials, payment information, special-category personal data or information you are not authorised to disclose.

Why we use it

We use enquiry information to assess and respond to the request, take steps you ask us to take before a possible contract, protect the form from abuse and maintain a traceable record of the conversation. The relevant UK GDPR bases are steps requested before entering a contract and our legitimate interests in responding to genuine enquiries and securing the service. We do not add enquiry details to marketing lists without separate permission.

Who receives it

Form submissions are securely relayed through Cloudflare Pages Functions to Google Apps Script and Gmail to send the studio notification and your receipt. Cloudflare Turnstile processes browser and verification signals to check for abuse; the enquiry text passes through the Cloudflare relay to Google and is not included in the Turnstile verification request. The relay does not intentionally store or log enquiry bodies. Copy buttons prepare text locally in your browser; copying does not send a message. You choose whether to paste and send it through another service. The public site is hosted through Cloudflare Pages and mirrored on GitHub Pages; those providers may process technical request logs under their own terms. Direct WhatsApp and WeChat enquiries are processed by the relevant messaging provider.

Some providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. We rely on the transfer mechanisms and safeguards offered by those providers. We do not sell enquiry information.

How long we keep it

If an enquiry does not become active work, we normally delete or anonymise the correspondence within 12 months after the last meaningful contact. We may keep a record longer where it is necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, resolve abuse or comply with law. If an enquiry becomes a contract, project and accounting records are retained separately for the periods required by contract and applicable law.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal information, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier processing. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Security, cookies and automation

The public studio site does not currently use advertising cookies, behavioural analytics or automated decision-making. The form uses native validation, a hidden spam trap, Cloudflare Turnstile verification and server-side limits. The delivery handler stores reference numbers, hashed fingerprints and delivery states for replay prevention; records older than seven days are removed during subsequent processing. It does not store the enquiry text in that delivery ledger. Enquiry information is not used to make a solely automated decision with legal or similarly significant effects.

If AI-assisted lead classification is added later, it will be limited to routing or drafting, governed by data-minimisation and supplier review, and remain subject to human approval before any response.

Contact and complaints

Send privacy requests to keke19870699@gmail.com. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office through ico.org.uk.

This notice covers the Anna AI Studios company website. DateLens and FrameFetch are separate product surfaces and may publish their own product-specific notices. We will update this page when practices, providers or contact details change.
