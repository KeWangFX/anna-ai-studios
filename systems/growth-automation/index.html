02 — Pilot scope

What we agree
before a pilot

Choose one business constraint first. The scope, permitted data and human responsibilities are agreed before connecting a system to live customers.

Team and first workflow
For service teams with recurring calls, feedback or website enquiries and a person responsible for follow-up. Start with one system; a combined rollout is separately scoped.
What you prepare
Approved business information, example enquiries, current volumes and handoff rules. Confirm account ownership, data permissions and the people responsible for approvals and customer follow-up.
Proposed pilot deliverables
List the workflow or pages, agreed connections, test records and operating handover in the proposal. Supported countries, languages, phone providers and CRM or booking tools require assessment for your environment.
Acceptance checks
Agree representative enquiries, expected actions, exception handling and recorded events. Test the full route to a responsible person; compare operational measures against the baseline without assuming revenue gains.
Operation and third-party costs
Agree responsibility for content updates, monitoring, complaints and provider accounts. You approve provider accounts and budgets; third-party model, telephone and hosting usage is itemised separately.
Assessment and next step
Use the relevant system link, or request a review if the first step is unclear. Scope, timeline, pricing and support arrangements are confirmed in writing after assessment; submitting the form does not confirm a booking or start delivery.
